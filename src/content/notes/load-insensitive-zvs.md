## Define the claim before evaluating it

“Load-insensitive” can describe different things: a broad soft-switching region, weak motion of a boundary, or a control strategy that maintains a switching condition. Those meanings are not interchangeable.

The completed note will therefore state the switching event, required charge transfer, operating variables, and range over which the claim is evaluated.

## Follow the trajectory

An impedance trajectory can show how the network moves as coupling, frequency, or load changes. A useful diagram must also label which portion of that trajectory corresponds to the commutation condition being discussed.

### Inspect the light-load edge

Light load is not merely a smaller version of the nominal point. Available commutation current, parasitic charge, dead time, and control authority can interact differently near the boundary.
